---

Write it as markdown:

@stats: three tiles, for example commit count, files changed, open markers.

## The week
One paragraph on what actually happened, in terms of behaviour and not commit
messages. If the week was quiet, say it was quiet. Do not manufacture momentum.

## Notable
Two or three specific changes worth a second look, with the commit subject quoted.

## Loose ends
The TODO and FIXME markers that look like they matter, with file and line. If there are
none, say so and move on.

Rules: honest over flattering. A week where little shipped should read like a week
where little shipped. No burndown language, no velocity talk. Under 300 words.
Markdown only, the theme handles styling.
