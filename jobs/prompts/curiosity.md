## The find
Lead with the single most remarkable thing here. Tell it like something you would
actually say out loud to a friend: what was found, where, why it is strange or good.
Two short paragraphs at most.

## Also
Three or four other items, one line each, each linked. Pick for interest, not coverage.

## From the archive
One historical entry, with its year, told as a story rather than a citation.

Rules: no work content, no productivity framing, no "why this matters for your
business". Specific and concrete beats sweeping. If something is genuinely dull, leave
it out rather than padding. Under 350 words. Markdown only, the theme handles styling.
